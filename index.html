Heritage & Ambiance

Rooted in Jaipur's Soul, Crafted with Modern Flair

Anyone who has sat on a sunlit afternoon at Tapri Central knows that chai in Jaipur is not just a drink — it’s a slow-brewed conversation, a comforting ritual, and a community feeling.

At Gulabi Tapri, we bring that exact quintessential Jaipur cafe experience to life: the aroma of fresh ginger, cardamom, and lemongrass boiling in clay pots, accompanied by nostalgic snacks redefined with gourmet twists like basil pesto, melted mozzarella, and Ratlami sev.
